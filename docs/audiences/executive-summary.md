# Executive Summary

!!! abstract "AutoAgentAI — one page"
    An autonomous AI agent that captures and uses the **most valuable,
    least-captured data in the car industry** — the buyer's real pre-purchase intent
    and decision behaviour — running **privately on-prem on local AI models**, and
    **closing the loop from the showroom floor to the OEM.**

**The problem.** Buying a car is a huge, considered, emotional decision, yet the data
about *why* buyers choose, hesitate, or walk is almost entirely uncaptured — because
it's structurally impossible for salespeople to capture. The industry runs on memory,
sticky notes, lagging sales numbers, and survey-with-a-prize guesswork.

**Why now.** Three curves crossed at once: **agentic AI** can finally capture and
reason over this signal; **open local models** make it private and cheap enough to
run on-prem; and **tightening privacy law** makes sovereign, on-prem data the only
durable way to hold it.

**What it is.** One agent across four jobs — unify customer data, model behaviour,
generate custom insights, act on them — delivered through McDonald's-style kiosks and
iPad nodes, running on the dealership's own hardware so **customer data never leaves
the building.**

**The wedge.** This data scores at the top on every value driver: huge stakes per
decision, a lifelong repeating relationship, genuine decision signal (not vanity
metrics), near-total scarcity (nobody captures it), and value at **every** level —
salesperson, dealer, group, and manufacturer.

**The moat.** The AI is commodity; the defensibility is **capture + privacy + the
loop** — the on-floor footprint that captures the signal, the on-prem posture that
can legally hold it, and the aggregated feedback loop to the OEM that compounds as it
scales.

**Business model.** Four tiers as a pricing ladder: land with low-cost **Tier 0**
cloud pilots, expand to on-prem **Tiers 1–3** (hardware + recurring license), multiply
across dealer groups, and — longer term — monetize the **OEM-facing** buyer-intent
insight product.

**The impact on the floor.** Fewer leads lost, reps who know the customer before they
walk up, more trade-ins and service retention, and a salesperson's role that shifts
from pressure-and-admin to **assurance and assistance** — a leaner, sharper team, each
person amplified by the agent.

**Status & ask.** Concept and documentation complete; next is specifications plus a
measured design-partner pilot. **We're seeking** a funding round *(amount to
confirm)*, a **design-partner dealership**, and an **OEM introduction** to scope the
feedback loop.
